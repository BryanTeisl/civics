    Local Measures 101

    Ordinances: Local laws enacted by a city council or county board (for example, zoning rules, municipal codes, and tax/fee updates). Typically require multiple readings before final adoption.

    Resolutions: Formal policy statements, contract approvals, intergovernmental agreements, or budget authorizations that usually pass in a single meeting.

    Impact Score (0–100): A transparent, auditable score weighing fiscal/budget scope, citywide vs. localized reach, contested vote margins, and structural policy change—never partisan ideology.
