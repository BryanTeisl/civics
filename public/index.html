    Local Measures 101

    Ordinances: Local laws enacted by a city council or county board (zoning rules, municipal codes, and tax/fee updates). Typically require multiple readings before final adoption.

    Resolutions: Formal policy statements, contract approvals, or budget authorizations that usually pass in a single meeting.

    Consent Agenda: Routine items grouped together and approved in a single unanimous vote unless a councilmember pulls an item for separate discussion.

    Impact Score (0–100): A deterministic, nonpartisan score weighing Legal Binding Weight (10–40 pts), Contestedness / Vote Margin (0–40 pts), and Body Turnout (0–20 pts).
