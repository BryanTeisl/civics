Plain-English Guide

Local Government Measures 101

📜 Ordinance (Binding Local Law)

A permanent city or county law — such as zoning rules, local taxes, speed limits, or housing regulations. Usually requires two readings and a public hearing.

📝 Resolution (Policy or Funding Action)

A formal decision approving contracts, budgets, grants, or official policy statements without permanently rewriting the municipal code.

🏙️ City Council vs. County Commissioners

City Councils govern local services inside city limits (zoning, city roads, police, parks). County Commissioners govern county-wide services (elections, sheriff, property assessments, public health, and unincorporated areas).

⚖️ How Vote Impactfulness (0–100) Is Calculated

Every measure is scored transparently using three objective factors — never subjective political ratings:

  • Legal Weight (up to 40 pts): Binding Ordinances & Budgets score ~35 pts; Resolutions & Contracts score ~25 pts.
  • Contestedness (up to 40 pts): Split votes (e.g., 4–3) score high; unanimous votes (e.g., 4–0) add 0 pts.
  • Turnout (up to 20 pts): Full-council roll calls score higher than small sub-committee votes.

A Pivotal Vote means that official was on the winning side of a 1-vote margin.
