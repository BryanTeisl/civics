# CivicPulse Outreach & Voluntary Support Templates

## 1. Community & Civic Group Post (Nextdoor, Reddit, Neighborhood Associations)
**Subject / Headline:** Free tool to see how your City Council actually votes (Fort Collins & Denver live)

Hi neighbors — I built a free, nonpartisan web tool called **CivicPulse** to make it easy to see what our local City Council and County Commissioners are actually voting on without digging through 200-page meeting PDFs.

- **Click your neighborhood on the map** to find your local councilmember and county commissioners
- **Read plain-English vote summaries** with direct links back to the official city records
- **Search any local topic** (housing, zoning, transit, water, budgets) across recent meetings

It is **100% free for individuals**. Right now Fort Collins, Denver, and Larimer County are live — if your town isn't tracked yet (like Loveland, Boulder, Longmont, or Greeley), just click your town on the map to request it so I know where to expand next:
👉 https://civics-d03.pages.dev/public/
